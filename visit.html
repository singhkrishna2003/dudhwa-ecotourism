Plan Your Visit to Dudhwa

Everything you need to know before visiting Dudhwa National Park — from best seasons to safari timings and essential guidelines.

Park Timings

Open: November 15 to June 15 annually

Closed during monsoon season (July–October) for animal breeding and forest regeneration.

Best Time to Visit

November to February (Peak Season)

  • Pleasant weather (10–25°C)
  • Best wildlife sightings
  • Ideal for families and international tourists
  • Higher accommodation rates

March to April (Excellent Season)

  • Warm but comfortable (20–35°C)
  • Excellent tiger sightings near waterholes
  • Great for photography
  • Moderate crowd levels

May to Mid-June (Summer Season)

  • Hot weather (30–40°C)
  • Maximum wildlife concentration near water sources
  • Best for serious wildlife enthusiasts
  • Lower tourist numbers

Note: Carry warm clothing for winter visits (December–February) as temperatures can drop significantly at night.

Safari Timings

Winter Season (November 1 – March 31)

Morning Safari: 6:30 AM – 9:00 AM

Evening Safari: 2:30 PM – 5:30 PM

Summer Season (April 1 – June 15)

Morning Safari: 5:30 AM – 8:30 AM

Evening Safari: 3:30 PM – 6:30 PM

Morning safaris offer the best chances for wildlife sightings as animals are most active during cooler hours.

Entry Requirements

Documents Required

  • Valid government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Passport, Driving License)
  • Same ID must be presented at the park entrance

Entry Fees (Subject to Change)

  • Indian Nationals: ₹60 per person
  • Foreign Nationals: ₹150 per person
  • Vehicle Entry: ₹250 per vehicle
  • Still Camera: ₹250
  • Video Camera: Additional charges may apply

Important Guidelines

Do’s

  • Arrive 15 minutes before safari time
  • Follow guide instructions at all times
  • Maintain silence during wildlife viewing
  • Wear neutral-colored clothing
  • Carry water, snacks, and sun protection
  • Bring binoculars

Don’ts

  • Do not get down from the vehicle
  • No loud noises or music
  • No littering inside the park
  • No smoking or alcohol
  • No feeding animals
  • No bright or shiny clothing

✈️ 🚆 🚗 Travel Guide

Getting to Dudhwa National Park

Dudhwa National Park is well-connected to major cities across North India. The nearest town is Palia Kalan, which serves as the base for most visitors.

By Air

Nearest Airport: Chaudhary Charan Singh International Airport, Lucknow (221 km)

Flight Connectivity: Daily flights from Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Kolkata, Patna, and other major cities via Air India, IndiGo, SpiceJet, and Vistara.

From Airport to Dudhwa:

  • Taxi: 4–5 hours drive (approximately ₹4,000–6,000)
  • Pre-booked transfers through your resort
  • Self-drive rental cars available

Alternative: Pantnagar Airport (160 km) has limited connectivity but is closer to the park.

By Train

Nearest Railway Stations:

  1. Dudhwa Railway Station (12 km)
    • Small station with limited trains
    • Suitable for connecting from nearby towns
  2. Mailani Railway Station (35 km)
    • Gateway for Kishanpur Wildlife Sanctuary
    • Better connectivity to major cities
    • Regular trains from Delhi, Lucknow, Bareilly

From Railway Station: Taxis and auto-rickshaws are available. It is recommended to pre-arrange resort pickup.

By Road

From Delhi

450 km • 8–9 hours

Route: NH-9 via Moradabad, Bareilly, Lakhimpur Kheri

Regular buses available; best to drive or hire a taxi

From Lucknow

220 km • 4–5 hours

Route via Sitapur, Lakhimpur Kheri

State transport buses available; well-maintained roads

From Nainital

180 km • 4–5 hours

Scenic drive through the Terai region

Ideal for combining hill station and wildlife experience

From Bareilly

180 km • 3 hours

Shortest route from a major city with good road conditions

Bus Services: UPSRTC operates regular buses. Private luxury coaches are available from Delhi and Lucknow. Resorts can arrange private transfers on request.

Local Transport

  • Within Park: Only authorized safari vehicles allowed
  • Palia Kalan to Park: Safari vehicles and taxis available
  • Between Zones: Arrange transport through your accommodation
